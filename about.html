OUR STORY

Your trusted partner for scrap collection

ScrapAdda started with a simple idea: selling scrap should be easy, fair and transparent. We connect households, offices, shops and factories with verified collection partners and certified recyclers.

Every kilogram is weighed in front of you on a digital scale, priced at the daily market rate, and paid instantly. What we collect is sent for responsible recycling so that it never ends up in a landfill.

  • Digital weighing, no cheating
  • Trained & verified pickup team
  • Instant cash or UPI payment
  • 100% responsible recycling
